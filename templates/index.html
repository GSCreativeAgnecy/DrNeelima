About Dr. Neelima Talari

With over 15+ years of experience in Obstetrics & Gynecology, Dr. Neelima Talari is a highly qualified and compassionate gynecologist dedicated to women's health and well-being.

She specializes in managing twin and triplet pregnancies, performs advanced gynecological and obstetric surgeries, and provides comprehensive maternity care with a patient-first approach.

Dr. Neelima offers consultation services at multiple hospitals and is committed to ensuring every woman receives the highest standard of personalized healthcare.
